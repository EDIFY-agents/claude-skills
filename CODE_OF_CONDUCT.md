# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be direct about code. Be decent about people. Criticism of an idea, a design, or
a pull request is welcome and often the most useful thing in the thread —
criticism of a person is not.

## Standards

Expected:

- Technical directness. "This is wrong, and here is why" is a good comment.
- Assuming competence. The person on the other end is usually solving a problem
  you cannot see.
- Accepting a decision once it is made, in public, with reasons.

Unacceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery; unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or bad-faith argument after a decision
  has been explained.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments based on any personal characteristic.

## Scope

Applies to issues, discussions, pull requests, commit messages, and any space
where someone represents this project.

## Enforcement

Report to **conduct@edify.dev**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept
confidential.

Responses escalate as the situation warrants: a private correction, a public
correction, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who do not follow
this in good faith face the same consequences as anyone else.
