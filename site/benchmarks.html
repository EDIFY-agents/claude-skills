Benchmarks

Same model. Same repo. Same task.

The comparison that matters is not against a strawman. It is against a modern, well-configured agent with a good prompt, and against a free alternative people actually use.

01 / Status

Methodology and results will be public, including the runs where EDIFY loses. Until real, reproducible numbers exist, every cell below stays a dash. A benchmark filled in before the benchmark ran is the fastest way to lose a technical audience.

02 / The three arms

What each column means.

AAgent aloneThe same model, in auto mode, with a good prompt and no harness. The baseline that matters, because it is what you are doing today.
BSpec KitThe closest free alternative in the same shape. A good tool, and the honest comparison.
CEDIFY/spec → /plan → /tasks → /build → /verify, with the graph. If this does not beat arm A, the product has no reason to exist, and the page will say so.
03 / The minimum credible package

What has to be true before a number goes up.

Seven conditions. Every one of them is checkable in the public repository when the runs land.

2–3 non-trivial open-source repositories, with real history and more than one language.
10–20 varied tasks: features, a refactor, a bug with a wrong first hypothesis, a migration.
The same model version, opening prompt and limits across all three arms.
Measured: task success, tests, human interventions, retries, tokens and cost, wall-clock time.
Scripts, prompts and raw transcripts published in benchmarks/.
A short video of a visible failure case.
Case studies from design partners come after, not instead.
