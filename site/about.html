About

The same starting line, for everyone.

A four-person team and a thousand-engineer company already have the same models. What neither had was the structure that makes an agent's output something you can trust on a real codebase. EDIFY is that structure, and the full version is free.

Edifice

The structure

Built to be inspected and to outlast the session that made it. A graph, a spec, a plan, a task list and a verification report, all as files in your repository. Readable by the next engineer, the next model, and the next reviewer.

Edify

To instruct

To make someone more capable than they were. EDIFY does not replace the agent you use. It teaches it your repository, then checks its work.

What we will not trade

Six rules the product is held to.

V1Prove it, do not promise itEvery claim on this site is backed by the CLI you can install today. If we cannot show the mechanism, we do not make the claim.
V2The engineer is the protagonistMore capability for the person accountable for the change. Never more autonomy for the model.
V3Name the limit firstThe sentence that admits a boundary earns the three that follow it. Degraded is printed in amber, not hidden.
V4Files, not conversationsNothing important lives in a chat. The next session reads the decision instead of re-making it.
V5Your code stays yoursNo telemetry, no account, no server. Local-first is a property we defend, not a tier.
V6Open to inspectionThe source is readable, the graph is plain text, and every installed file carries its origin and hash.
Known limitations

What EDIFY does not cover yet, said before you find it.

Languages other than Python are line-scanned unless Universal Ctags is installed.graph
The benchmark has not finished running. No performance claim is made until it has.benchmarks
Nothing is enforced at runtime. A hard gate is --exit-code in CI you own.verification
The first three commands are slow on purpose. For a small task, that is ceremony; skip EDIFY for it.workflow
A single-vendor project. Every version still converts to Apache 2.0 on schedule.governance
