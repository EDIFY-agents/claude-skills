Why these limits

Each gate is where a team's need differs from a trial's.

Nothing working was removed to create a reason to pay.

25,000 nodes is roughly a 200–300k-line repository. Below it, EDIFY is genuinely optional. Above it is who the product is for.graph.unlimited
